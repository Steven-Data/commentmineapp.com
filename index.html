CommentMine

留言礦 · 把你的 YouTube 留言變成下一支影片的靈感

An iOS app for YouTube creators. It reads the comments on your own channel and turns the questions viewers keep asking into evidence-backed video idea cards, ranked by how hot they are.

Coming soon to the App Store · 即將上架
1

Connect your channel

Sign in with Google using a read-only YouTube permission. CommentMine only looks; it can't post, reply, or change anything.

2

We mine the comments

Duplicates are removed, Chinese and English are handled together, and comments asking the same thing are grouped into one idea.

3

Pick an idea

Each card shows a title, a hook, an outline, the real comments behind it quoted word for word, and a reply draft you can copy.

What CommentMine will never do. It never posts, replies, likes, or edits anything on YouTube. Reply drafts are for you to copy and review. Every idea card cites at least one real comment, quoted verbatim, so nothing is invented. Deleting your account removes your channel data, comments, idea cards, and access tokens from our servers. YouTube access tokens are never stored on your phone.

CommentMine 絕不會做的事。不會在 YouTube 上發文、回覆、按讚或修改任何內容；回覆草稿只供你複製與確認。每張靈感卡至少引用一則真實留言的原文，不會憑空捏造。刪除帳號會一併移除伺服器上的頻道資料、留言、靈感卡與存取權杖；YouTube 權杖不會存放在手機上。

Who it's for · 適合誰

Creators with an active comment section who want to know what their audience is actually asking for, in Traditional Chinese, English, or both. CommentMine only ever analyzes your own channel. It does not look at other creators' channels.

適合留言區活躍、想知道觀眾真正在問什麼的創作者，支援繁體中文與英文。CommentMine 只分析你自己的頻道，不會看其他創作者的頻道。
